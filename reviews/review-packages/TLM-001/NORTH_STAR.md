# North Star Digest

Canonical sources: `vision/NORTH_STAR.md`, `metrics/success-metrics.md`.

## Mission

Give every school teacher back the time and clarity to teach every student well.

## Vision

No teacher loses evenings to repetitive prep and fragmented tools; every student gets timely, personalised attention under a trusted teacher amplified by AI.

## Product Promise

**Prepare with confidence. Teach with clarity. Follow up with insight — in less time than today.**

## Primary users

Teacher (P0) → Student (P1) → Parent (P2) → Principal (P3)

## Core value proposition

For overloaded teachers, EduVijna’s **Teacher OS** turns one **Teaching Intent** into ready-to-review kits — inheriting **School Context** and **Teacher Memory** — then compounds value through the **Daily Loop** (Prepare → Teach → Observe → Assess → Analyze → Improve).

Unlike WhatsApp + Word + YouTube + paper registers + disconnected generators.

## Headline metric

**Hours returned to teaching per teacher per week**

Twin: **% of AI outputs approved with ≤2 edits**

## Metric set to track

Preparation time · Assessment creation time · Weekly hours saved · Teacher satisfaction · Student engagement · Feature adoption · Retention · School adoption · Learning follow-through · Trust/control guardrails
