| 01 | Lesson prep rebuild | 6–10 hrs/week | High |
| 02 | Slow assessment creation | 2–4 hrs/week | High |

## Rollup

- Typical weekly loss: **~20–28 hours**  
- Extraordinary target: return **8–12 hours/week** with quality intact  

## Review implication

Do not ship disconnected generators that add tool fragmentation (PAIN-05). Unify under teaching intent.
