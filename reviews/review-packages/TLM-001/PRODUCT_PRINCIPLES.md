# Product Principles Digest

Canonical source: `vision/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`.

## Principles (v0.2)

1. Teacher First  
2. AI Assists, Teacher Decides  
3. One Intent, Many AI Services (**Teaching Intent API**)  
4. Minimize Teacher Workload  
5. Explain Every AI Output  
6. Human Approval Before Student Delivery  
7. Save Time Daily  
8. Continuous Improvement (via **Teacher Memory**)  
9. Privacy by Design  
10. School-Aware AI (**expanded School Context inheritance**)  
11. **Teacher Memory Compounds**  
12. **Daily Loop Is the Product**

## Conflict resolution order

1. Student safety and privacy  
2. Teacher control and approval  
3. Workload reduction  
4. Insight quality  
5. Speed of delivery
