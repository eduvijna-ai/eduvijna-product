---

## North Star

**Hours returned to teaching per teacher per week.**

**Twin:** % of AI outputs approved with ≤2 edits.

---

## Metric set

| Metric | Definition | Direction |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Teacher hours saved | Non-teaching academic hours saved / week | ↑ |
| Preparation time | Median minutes Intent → approved kit for one period | ↓ |
| Assessment time | Median minutes to approved assessment ready to conduct | ↓ |
| Student improvement | Learning follow-through: % weak concepts remediated ≤7 days; engagement on assigned work | ↑ |
| Teacher satisfaction | TSAT / NPS for Teacher OS | ↑ |
| Retention | Monthly active teacher retention; school renewal | ↑ |
| AI approval rate | % drafts approved (vs discarded); light-edit rate | ↑ quality |
| Adoption | % licensed teachers active ≥3 days/week in Prepare or Assess | ↑ |
| School adoption | % schools ≥60% teacher activation in 90 days | ↑ |
| Unauthorised publish rate | Student/parent deliveries without approval | → 0 |

---

## Phase emphasis

| Roadmap phase | Lead metrics |
|---------------|--------------|
| Foundation | Adoption of Today/Prepare, prep time, approval rate |
| Teaching Assistant | Hours saved, light-edit rate, Observe→Improve rate |
| Student Intelligence | Engagement, remediation follow-through |
| School Intelligence | School adoption, retention |
| Parent Intelligence | Approved comms usage without after-hours spike |
