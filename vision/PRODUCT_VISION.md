# Product Vision

**Status:** Draft — TLM-001  
**Owner:** EduVijna Product Office  
**Primary lens:** Teacher

---

## The problem we exist to solve

Indian school teachers carry the full weight of education — planning, teaching, assessing, mentoring, reporting, and communicating — while tools remain fragmented, paperwork remains heavy, and personalisation for every student remains nearly impossible at scale.

Teachers do not need more software.

Teachers need **time, clarity, and confidence** so they can teach.

---

## Vision statement

EduVijna becomes the **Teacher OS** for every school teacher — quietly removing administrative burden, accelerating preparation, deepening insight into every learner, and leaving the teacher firmly in control of every decision that reaches a student.

The Teacher OS is organised as:

**Today · Prepare · Teach · Assess · Improve · AI Assistant**

It runs on four product abstractions:

1. **Teaching Intent** — one goal → many orchestrated artefacts  
2. **Teacher Memory** — persistent profile that compounds over time  
3. **School Context** — automatic institutional inheritance on every request  
4. **Daily Loop** — Prepare → Teach → Observe → Assess → Analyze → Improve → Prepare Again

---

## Who we serve first

1. **Teachers** — primary users and primary value creators  
2. **Students** — ultimate beneficiaries of better teaching  
3. **Parents** — partners in learning continuity  
4. **Principals / school leaders** — sponsors of school-wide adoption  

Teacher value is the entry point. School value follows. Platform value follows school value.

---

## What extraordinary value looks like

A teacher who uses EduVijna should feel:

- Prepared for tomorrow in minutes, not hours  
- Clear about who is struggling and why  
- Free from repetitive paperwork  
- Confident that AI suggestions are accurate, explainable, and board-aligned  
- In control — never overridden by automation  

---

## Product posture

EduVijna is not a content dump.  
EduVijna is not a chatbot bolted onto school software.  
EduVijna is not an ERP that occasionally “adds AI.”

EduVijna is a **teacher-first operating system for teaching life** — where AI assists the Daily Loop, Teaching Intent replaces tool menus, Teacher Memory ends first-time-assistant behaviour, School Context ends re-typing institutional facts, and ERP systems stay connected as context — not as a parallel chore.
