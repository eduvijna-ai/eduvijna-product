# North Star

**Status:** Draft — TLM-001  
**Owner:** EduVijna Product Office  
**Horizon:** 3–5 years, measured quarterly

---

## Mission

Give every school teacher back the time and clarity to teach every student well.

---

## Vision

A world where no Indian school teacher loses evenings to repetitive preparation, paperwork, and fragmented tools — and where every student receives timely, personalised attention guided by a trusted teacher amplified by AI.

---

## Product Promise

**“Prepare with confidence. Teach with clarity. Follow up with insight — in less time than today.”**

EduVijna promises to:

1. Compress preparation without lowering quality  
2. Surface learning gaps before they become failures  
3. Keep teachers in control of every student-facing artefact  
4. Connect classroom work to school life (attendance, exams, parents) without double work  

---

## Primary Users

| Priority | User | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| P0 | Teacher | Owns preparation, instruction, assessment, and student outcomes |
| P1 | Student | Experiences the quality of teaching and follow-up |
| P2 | Parent | Extends learning support at home |
| P3 | Principal / School Leader | Enables adoption, standards, and school outcomes |

---

## Core Value Proposition

**For teachers** who are overloaded with preparation, assessment, and administrative work,  
**EduVijna** is the teaching companion that turns one teaching intent into ready-to-review plans, materials, assessments, and insights —  
**unlike** fragmented WhatsApp + Word + YouTube + paper registers + disconnected generators —  
**because** it is organised as a Teacher OS on Teaching Intent, Teacher Memory, School Context, and the Daily Loop — explainable and teacher-controlled.

---

## Long-term Vision

By year 5, EduVijna is the default teaching operating layer in partner schools:

- Teachers plan a week in under an hour of focused review  
- Assessments are created, administered, and analysed in a continuous loop  
- Every student has a living learning profile visible to their teacher  
- Parents receive meaningful, multilingual updates without teacher burnout  
- Principals see teaching health — not vanity dashboards  
- AI remains an assistant; professional judgement remains human

## Success Metrics (North Star set)

### Teacher outcomes

| Metric | Definition | Direction |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Teacher preparation time | Median minutes to prepare one full period (plan + materials + assessment) | ↓ |
| Assessment creation time | Median minutes to create a board-aligned quiz/worksheet ready for review | ↓ |
| Weekly hours saved | Self-reported + measured reduction in non-teaching academic admin hours / week | ↑ saved |
| Teacher satisfaction (TSAT) | Periodic teacher NPS / satisfaction with EduVijna teaching support | ↑ |
| Teacher retention of habit | % of active teachers using core prep/assess flows ≥3 days/week | ↑ |

### Student outcomes (teacher-mediated)

| Metric | Definition | Direction |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Student engagement | Attempt rate, completion, and timely submission on assigned work | ↑ |
| Learning follow-through | % of identified weak concepts that receive remediation within 7 days | ↑ |
| Assessment integrity confidence | Teacher-rated confidence that assessments measure intended outcomes | ↑ |

### Product & school outcomes

| Metric | Definition | Direction |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Feature adoption | % of licensed teachers adopting priority capabilities (prep, assess, insight, communicate) | ↑ |
| Retention | Teacher monthly active retention; school annual renewal | ↑ |
| School adoption | Schools with ≥60% teacher activation within 90 days of onboarding | ↑ |

---

## North Star Metric (single headline)

**Hours returned to teaching per teacher per week.**

Supporting twin: **% of AI outputs approved with ≤2 edits** (quality + trust proxy).

---

## Non-goals (explicit)

- Replace teachers  
- Autonomously message parents without approval (default)  
- Optimise for content volume over classroom usefulness  
- Build AI demos disconnected from school calendars and roles  

---

## Measurement principles

1. Prefer observed time and behaviour over vanity feature counts  
2. Separate *generation* from *approved delivery*  
3. Measure school cohorts, not only power users  
4. Track burden reduction and learning impact together  

Detailed operational definitions live in `metrics/success-metrics.md`.
