# Student Persona

**ID:** PERSONA-STUDENT-001  
**Status:** Draft — supporting persona for Teacher Journey Model  
**Codename:** Kabir

---

## Snapshot

Kabir is a Grade 7 student. He wants to do well, but attention and confidence vary by subject. He experiences school as a sequence of periods, homework, tests, and adult expectations. He rarely sees the “system” — he feels whether learning is clear, fair, and encouraging.

---

## Goals

- Understand today’s lesson without feeling lost  
- Finish homework in reasonable time  
- Improve marks in weaker subjects  
- Feel noticed when he tries  
- Avoid public embarrassment when he doesn’t know something  

## Daily reality

- 6–8 periods, uneven energy  
- Copies notes; may not grasp concepts in real time  
- Homework from multiple teachers the same evening  
- Tests announced with short notice sometimes  
- Uses phone for YouTube explanations more than for school apps  

## Frustrations

- Teaching too fast or too abstract  
- Homework that repeats without helping  
- Feedback that is only a mark, not guidance  
- Being compared publicly  
- Not knowing what to revise before exams  

## Relationship to teacher product

EduVijna helps students **through teachers**: clearer lessons, better-targeted practice, faster feedback, and remediation that arrives before despair.

Students are beneficiaries first, direct users second (practice, assigned quizzes, learning materials).

## Success definition

> “I know what I got wrong, what to practise next, and I don’t feel stupid asking.”
