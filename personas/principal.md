# Principal Persona

**ID:** PERSONA-PRINCIPAL-001  
**Status:** Draft — supporting persona for Teacher Journey Model  
**Codename:** Mr. Rao

---

## Snapshot

Mr. Rao leads a K–12 school of ~1,200 students. He cares about academic outcomes, inspections, parent reputation, and teacher stability. He sponsors edtech only when it improves teaching quality and school operations without creating chaos or compliance risk.

---

## Goals

- Consistent syllabus coverage and exam readiness across sections  
- Reliable academic records (attendance, marks, reports)  
- Teachers who feel supported, not surveilled  
- Parents who trust school communication  
- Smooth admissions, events, and inspection readiness  
- Measurable improvement without burning out staff  

## Frustrations

- Tools teachers won’t adopt  
- Parallel registers and conflicting data  
- Surprise parent escalations  
- Uneven teaching quality across sections  
- “AI pilots” that demos well and dies in week three  

## Relationship to teacher product

Principals buy and mandate; teachers determine whether the product lives.  
EduVijna must show **school-level outcomes** rooted in **teacher daily value**.

## Success definition

> “My teachers prepare better in less time, records are trustworthy, and parents stop saying they were not informed.”

## Adoption stance

- Needs board alignment and privacy clarity  
- Needs training that fits staff meeting time, not week-long workshops  
- Needs evidence within one academic term
