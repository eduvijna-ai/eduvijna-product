# Parent Persona

**ID:** PERSONA-PARENT-001  
**Status:** Draft — supporting persona for Teacher Journey Model  
**Codename:** Meera

---

## Snapshot

Meera is a working parent of a Grade 7 student. She wants visibility without stalking the teacher. She trusts the school when communication is timely, clear, and respectful — and loses trust when updates are late, contradictory, or only arrive after a crisis.

---

## Goals

- Know if her child is attending, coping, and improving  
- Understand homework and test schedules  
- Help at home without re-teaching the entire syllabus  
- Receive early warnings, not surprise failures at PTM  
- Communicate with teachers without awkwardness or blame  

## Current channels

- WhatsApp class groups  
- School diary  
- Phone calls  
- PTM  
- Occasional ERP parent login (if enabled)

## Frustrations

- Message floods and noise in class groups  
- Not knowing which topics are weak  
- Last-minute project / chart demands  
- Feeling judged when asking questions  
- Reports that show marks but not next steps  

## Relationship to teacher product

Parent value must **reduce teacher load**, not increase after-hours messaging.  
AI-drafted updates succeed only when teachers approve tone and timing.

## Success definition

> “I know how my child is doing this fortnight, and I know one useful thing I can do at home.”
